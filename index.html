Our Story

From a food trader to Seychelles’ integrated service provider

GSC started in 2005 as a food trading business serving hotels, restaurants and caterers (the HORECA sector). We were licensed as Global Supply Centre Pty Ltd in 2006.

Today our five business verticals, with bases in Mahé and Praslin, make us a single partner for the supplies, logistics and projects that island businesses depend on.

Who does GSC Group supply?

We supply hotels, resorts, restaurants, caterers, retailers and businesses across Seychelles.
